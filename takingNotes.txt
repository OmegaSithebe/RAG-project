# RAG Project - Day 1 - Case Study

# What is RAG?

**RAG = Retrieval Augmented Generation**

Think of RAG as giving an AI assistant an **open-book exam** instead of a **closed-book exam**.

### Without RAG

User asks:

> "What insurance products does Insurellm offer for small businesses?"
> 

The LLM answers based on its training data.

Problems:

- It may not know Insurellm.
- Information may be outdated.
- It may hallucinate (make things up).

---

### With RAG

User asks:

> "What insurance products does Insurellm offer for small businesses?"
> 

The system:

1. Searches company documents.
2. Finds relevant information.
3. Sends that information to the LLM.
4. LLM answers using the retrieved documents.

Result:

- More accurate
- Company-specific answers
- Less hallucination
- Low cost

---

# Real World Analogy

Imagine a new employee joins an insurance company.

You ask:

> "What is our cyber insurance coverage limit?"
> 

The employee has two choices:

### Option 1: Guess

They try to remember.

Result:

❌ Wrong answer possible

---

### Option 2: Check company documents

They search:

- Product manuals
- Policy documents
- Internal wiki

Then answer.

Result:

✅ Accurate answer

This is exactly what RAG does.


# What Problem Are We Solving?

We work for:

**Insurellm – An Insurance Technology Company**

Employees ask questions every day:

- What products do we sell?
- What are policy limits?
- What is the claim process?
- What are company regulations?
- What are customer eligibility requirements?

Hundreds of documents contain the answers.

Instead of employees manually searching documents:

👉 We build an AI Assistant.

---

# Project Goal

Build:

### Expert Knowledge Worker AI Assistant

The assistant should:

- Understand insurance-related questions
- Search company knowledge
- Find relevant information
- Give accurate answers

Example:

---

Employee asks:

> What is the waiting period for our health insurance plan?
> 

RAG searches:

```
health_policy
employee_manual
insurance_products
```

Finds:

```
Waiting period = 90 days
```

LLM responds:

> According to the Health Insurance Product Guide, the waiting period is 90 days.
